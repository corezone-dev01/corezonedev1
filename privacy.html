Privacy policy

What we collect

Your name, email address, optional company, WhatsApp number and budget range, the service you're interested in, and the project description you send through the inquiry form.

Why we collect it

Only to reply to your inquiry and discuss your project. We don't use your details for unrelated marketing without your permission.

Where it's stored

In a private Google Sheet, with a notification email sent to the business owner. If you choose to book a call or continue on WhatsApp, those services handle your details under their own policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep inquiries only as long as needed to respond and follow up. To request access to or deletion of your data, contact the email address listed on this site.
